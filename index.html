Two-time Grammy award winning artist and recent Grammy nominee Rickie Lee Jones is known as one of the most iconic and influential women singer-songwriters in rock and jazz music.

In “Welcome to Coolsville”, filmmaker Michael Gramaglia uses Jones’ extraordinary recent memoir to craft a hybrid, beautifully cinematic biography. A story that is as the author describes it, “Part Wizard of Oz, part Huck Finn.”

Logline

A runaway girl with a poet’s soul embarks on an unbelievable, Pulp Fiction-esque, rollercoaster ride through a magical and gritty 1970s America, ending up in L.A. with her unlikely transformation into a star.

Taking us from the other worldly backdrop of nascent Phoenix, Arizona of her youth, to her early teens spent in hitchhiking through the landscape of California’s counter-culture byways. From her artistic awakening as a songwriter in the creative enclave of 70s Venice Beach, to haunting the empty historic Troubadour with the likes of Tom Waits and Chuck E. Weiss. As she navigates the streets of L.A. in Chevys and old Cadillacs, riding with romantic and creative co-conspirators like Lowell George and Dr. John, we are carried along with her on her meteoric rise to stardom.

Powered by a hypnotic and introspective narrative voice, over images of magical realism, we ride shotgun on her surreal adventure. Leaning on a rich archive of performances, photos, demo recordings, enhanced by scenes of cinematic recreation, and found footage, a visually sweeping and spellbinding film emerges.

Production Team

Michael Gramaglia

Michael Gramaglia (Director) directed and produced the critically acclaimed and Grammy Award nominated documentary End of the Century: the Story of the Ramones and Don’t Ask Me Questions: the Unsung Life of Graham Parker. Michael was born into a family with a long film heritage. Early in his life he mentored at Cinecitta’ in Rome under Vittoria Storaro on Ishtar, Dick Tracy and The Sheltering Sky. Following those years, Michael had extensive behind the scenes experience in the east coast music industry. Soon after he combined his passions of film and music and made powerful films about extraordinary musical artists and their legacy.

He has also made numerous corporate films for I.B.M. that focus on the role of technology in music, art and humanity in the modern world; and smaller projects for artists such as Loudon Wainwright III, Blondie and Secret Machines.

Record Breaker Films

Is founded by award-winning producers Amy Hobby (What Happened, Miss Simone?, Secretary) and Lori Cheatle to produce music related documentary and narrative features and series with an eye to marketing and promotional music events.

Lori Cheatle

Lori Cheatle (Producer) is a Producer and Executive Producer of over 25 award-winning films and the founder of Hard Working Movies, a director-driven production company with an appetite for bold, beautiful, high-profile narratives. The films she’s produced have screened at all the top international film festivals, in cinemas and on TV and major platforms worldwide.
